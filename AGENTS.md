Our Mission & The Vibe
We are building "QuoteCatch," a premium, highly interactive web app widget. Our core goal is to turn standard, low-converting contractor websites (only roofers for now) into high-octane lead generation machines.

The Target Audience: We are building for the "Anti-CRM" home service contractor (starting with roofers). They don't want complex software; they just want more leads.
The Feeling: The app needs to feel incredibly modern, trustworthy, and fast. It should not look like a generic, boring contact form. It should feel like a premium, customized experience—smooth animations, large visual cards, and clear typography. It needs to build trust instantly with a homeowner.

The Story of the Application
Let's build this application as a story with two distinct sides: The Homeowner's Journey (The Widget) and The Contractor's Journey (The Dashboard).

Agent Directives: > Always prioritize mobile-first responsiveness, as 80% of homeowners will view the public widget on their phones.
